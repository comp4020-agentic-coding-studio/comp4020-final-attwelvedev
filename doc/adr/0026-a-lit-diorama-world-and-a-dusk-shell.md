# 26. A lit diorama world, a dusk shell, and motion that belongs to each object

## Status

proposed

This supersedes the "flat-shaded blocks" part of 0016 (`doc/adr/0016-tilted-three-quarter-world.md`). The
tilted 3/4 view, north up, the island over water and drawing everything in code all stand. The design
is `specs/2026-11-07-diorama-look.md`.

## Context

The user found the whole game "too simple/cartoonish" (backlog 2026-11-02). They had already approved a
realistic treatment on the keepsakes: banded metal gradients, a bevel, a soft south-east drop shadow,
every tone a world token mixed toward white or black, and flat under high contrast.

Looking at the screenshots, the shell and the world did not belong together:

- The navy shell is cold and the pastel world is warm.
- The canvas sits in a hard role-blue rectangle.
- The shell has no light and no depth.
- The radii and the heavy display face don't match the world's softness.

Animation was thin too. One expanding ring was drawn for seven different events. Two pairs (sequence ok
and wrong, plate press and release) differed only by colour, so Can't hear could not tell them apart
without colour, against DESIGN principle 6. The world's "rise" at the start was too subtle to read as a
tilt.

We weighed these:

- **A moody night-interior heist look.** A stronger change of identity, but "light means seen" would need
  rethinking, because the whole world would be dark. Rejected for now.
- **Decor placed by hand in room files.** Full control, but every room would need editing, with the risk
  of renumbering ids. Rejected for generated decor.
- **Redesigning the shell's layouts.** More than the problem asked. Rejected: a pass on material and
  colour only.
- **A vignette round the canvas.** Darkness means unseen, so it would read as mist. Rejected.

## Decision

- **World.**
  - A polished diorama, keeping Monument Valley's simplicity:
    - gradient-lit faces from the north-west
    - ambient occlusion and soft shadows
    - gloss on metal and glass
    - subtle stone or wood texture
  - Per-room wall tints from each room's `hue`.
  - Information stays flat: mist, night, cones, code swatches, outlines and labels.
  - High contrast drops all of it.
- **Decor.**
  - Generated by the client from a per-room theme on a fixed seed, from layout the client already has.
  - On wall faces and tops only. Never outlined, never animated.
  - Any glow is clipped to its own wall face, so it never lights the floor.
  - Nothing shaped like a game object or a motif.
- **Shell.** The same layouts, retinted to the world's dusk (night plum), with the world's light at low
  strength:
  - north-west panel gradients, a top sheen and a soft lift
  - softer radii
  - lit, filled buttons that keep their 2 px outline
  - the display face only for the few big lines
  - the canvas in a recessed bezel, with a role tab and a 1 px role rim
  - the dusk room, blurred, behind the reveal

  Every text colour keeps WCAG AA.
- **Motion.**
  - Each event animates its own object's physical action, so every sound's picture has its own
    silhouette.
  - The ring means only "station solved" and "you landed".
  - The start of a room is a staged ~2.4 s sequence: the flat plan, a visible tilt, a camera swoop to
    your start, then a landing. The server holds its first tick until it ends.

## Consequences

- DESIGN.md §1, §2, §4.5, §5 and §6 are rewritten. Five shell tokens and one world token are added.
- The world is drawn from a per-room tile atlas on an offscreen canvas (floor, walls with decor, glass,
  vents), so the gradients are drawn once. Frame time is measured on a phone profile and may not get
  worse.
- `Fx` gains a target object, and each drawer animates its own object in its own painter row and light
  clip.
- A test enforces that only the solved and landing events draw a ring. Another enforces that decor never
  paints a floor pixel.
- The room's clock starts about 2.4 s after all are ready. A server-side hold keeps the clients and the
  simulation in step.
- Several phases of work, each shipping a visible change with screenshots at 1280 and 375.
