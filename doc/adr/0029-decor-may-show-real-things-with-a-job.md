# 29. Decor may show real things with a job

## Status

proposed

This narrows the decor bullets of 0026 (`doc/adr/0026-a-lit-diorama-world-and-a-dusk-shell.md`), which
stay as history: decor is still generated by the client from the layout it already has, on wall faces
and tops only, never outlined, never animated, its glow clipped to its own face. What changes is what
decor may show. The plan is `plans/2026-11-08-decor-redesign.md`.

## Context

0026 banned anything "shaped like a game object or a motif", and DESIGN.md §4.9 turned that into a list
of shapes: no circle over 0.15 of a face, no more than two lines across one, no window, no lantern, no
single leaf. The painters that met it were abstract: planters and ferns standing on a wall's top, a
lattice, steel rectangles. In a playthrough on 2026-11-08 the user found they made no sense: pots on a
wall's top, "rectangles" in room 7 that could not be read, a thing on room 8's wall and notes that were
unclear. Abstract shapes failed the very test the ban was for: a shape you cannot name is one a player
may take for a signal.

## Decision

Decor shows real things you could name, each with a job, under seven rules (DESIGN.md §4.9):

1. **A job or a theme.** Every item lights, holds, hangs, covers or supports something, or belongs to
   the room's theme.
2. **Never a clue.** No text, numerals, note heads or motif shapes (moon, star, wave, key, eye, a single
   large leaf), and no pattern or colour that encodes a code.
3. **Never a game object.** Nothing that reads as a door, hatch, glass pane, slatted vent, bell, dial,
   wheel or gauge face, clock, keypad, camera or eye, a cone or beam, a lamp with rays, hazard stripes,
   or an exit or flag.
4. **Light is quiet.** Static, wall-mounted, one small source; the glow clipped to its own face, at or
   under 0.35 alpha, never on the floor.
5. **Windows look opaque**: shutters, curtains or a night-blue pane with muntins; never translucent, no
   sheen or glints, nothing in glass's colours.
6. **No outline in `WORLD.line`.** Depth from fills, lit from the north-west.
7. **High contrast draws no decor.** Placement is unchanged.

A wall's top carries only flush things (stone coping, moss, a row of rivets), never something that
would have to stand on it.

The guards move from shapes to rules: `decor-paint.test.ts` checks every painter for text, `WORLD.line`,
glass colours, gradients other than the glow, translucency and the glow's clip and alpha;
`decor.test.ts` checks kind names word by word against rule 3's list.

## Consequences

- Rooms read as places: a lantern on a bracket, a breaker cabinet, a violin hung by its scroll.
- The size-of-a-circle rule is gone, so a lantern's or a candle's shape is allowed; whether a shape
  reads as a signal is now judged by a person at review (the contact sheet, `review/decor/`), with the
  hard rules (text, outline, glass, glow) still checked by tests.
- More looks per kind means more atlas tiles; the frame bench decides whether the atlas cap moves.
- Every new kind has to be named so a person can say what it is; one that cannot be named is removed,
  not kept as filler.
