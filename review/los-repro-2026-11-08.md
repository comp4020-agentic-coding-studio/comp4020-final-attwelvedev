**Cause (c) refuted.** `cutToSight` in `sight.ts` is never called outside its own tests, and the server sends a
station's state (`shows`) whenever one of its display tiles is lit; for the bells the tune tile is a display tile.
The number seen alone (L4) is drawn by the client from a station it was sent whole; no field reached a seat that
could not perceive it, so L4 and L6 are a render bug, not a perception one.

**L4 and L6 go back to the user** (the plan: "If a symptom fits none of (a) to (c), stop and ask"). The fix is to
cut each part by its own piece only (a part's drawing under its own clip), which changes how every station is
drawn, not only the bells; that is beyond Task 4's files. Their tests are kept on the parked branch
`auto/overnight-parked-los-task4`.
