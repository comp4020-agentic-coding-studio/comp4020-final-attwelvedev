## What happened

This was the second informal playtest of my game with my pod in my crit. My pod had 6 members so we split into two lobbies. Both lobbies played only one room and it lasted about 5-10 minutes.

- Played only the first room in both teams
- Blind is "deadweight"
  - Furthermore, it just seems like blind is simply receiving instructions
- Blind was surprised that they couldn’t see anything, even though this is by design; they weren’t sure if this was as intended
- Again, like in playtest 1, mute just does their own thing
- Overall, players said it was fun and interesting
- Playing in real life encourages people talk naturally
  - The real-life setup doesn’t seem prominent enough and is glossed over
  - Players also glossed over the in-game comms options
- Again, the role assignment screen is unclear, with a player unsure what the different channels mean before playing
  - It was also unclear which players weren’t ready yet
  - Nor when someone left the lobby during this
- Players pointed out a popular game BOMBANANA, in which players do have role assignments that are based on their sensory deficits
  - E.g., blind being able to read braille

## What can be brought forward

What is currently working well includes the game concept itself and its gameplay implications just like in playtest 1. However, roles are not balanced yet, with blind being a deadweight, and mute being independent.
